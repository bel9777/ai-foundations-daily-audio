Jordan: Three main ways this goes wrong. First, you panic and delete all strong predictors. Just because borrower score strongly predicts approval doesn't mean it's a leak! You knew the credit score when they applied. The test is availability in time, not statistical strength.
Alex: Second mistake?
Jordan: Doing random splits when your problem is temporal. If you're predicting the future, train on the past and test on the future. Don't randomly scatter rows across time.
