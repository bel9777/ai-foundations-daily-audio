Jordan: The model slightly biases its word choices toward a specific pseudorandom pattern. To a human reader, the prose looks completely normal. But a detection algorithm can check whether those subtle patterns appear far more often than pure chance would allow. Paraphrasing or keeping texts very short usually degrades detection, though.
