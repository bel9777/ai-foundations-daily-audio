Jordan: The gradient is just a set of values that tells us how sensitive the loss score is to each parameter. In plain English, it answers: if I tweak this specific dial a tiny bit, does the error go up or down, and by how much?

Jordan: Yes. The height of the landscape at any point is the loss value. High areas are bad because they represent high error. Low valleys are good because they mean small error. Gradient descent pushes the model down off the high peaks and into those low valleys.
