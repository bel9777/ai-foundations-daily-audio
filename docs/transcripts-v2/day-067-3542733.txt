The danger, though, is falling into the correlation trap.
Alex: I know that one. Just because two things happen together doesn't mean one caused the other.
Jordan: Exactly. If a neuron lights up every time the model says Paris, it doesn’t mean that neuron is the "capital city" center. It might just be a "French word" sensor. It’s like surgically removing a wire to see if the machine stops working.
