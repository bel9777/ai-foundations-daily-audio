Alex: That leads straight into number four: distribution.
Jordan: Distribution is the overall landscape of situations in your data. If your real estate valuation model only trained on suburban Class A office parks, that is its distribution. Alex: And number five is the classic trap: data leakage.
Jordan: The absolute bane of production systems. Leakage happens when your training data accidentally includes information that wouldn't actually be available at the moment of the decision. Imagine trying to predict if a commercial lease deal will close, but your training spreadsheet includes a column called final legal signoff date.
Alex: Well, of course the model looks like a genius. If there's a signoff date, the deal closed!

Alex: You see this boundary between training experience and runtime experience clearly in the new Data agent OpenAI dropped for ChatGPT Work.
