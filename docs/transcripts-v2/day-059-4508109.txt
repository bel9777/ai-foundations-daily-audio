Alex: People always forget maintenance. A system that looks cheap per request on paper gets very expensive once two senior engineers spend half their week babysitting it.

Alex: Walk me through a concrete scenario. Say we are a startup building an AI tool that drafts customer support replies for agents.

Alex: So if you are just testing whether support agents even like AI drafts, buying is a no-brainer.
Jordan: Completely. Buy to validate the workflow. If volume goes through the roof and support becomes your company's core differentiator, you calculate the break-even point and consider building.
