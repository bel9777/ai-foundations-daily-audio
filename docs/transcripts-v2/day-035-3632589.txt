Jordan: Yes, context is the background information the model needs to make smart choices. That includes who the output is for, the situation, or key facts. Remember from day 34, a model can sound totally fluent while being completely wrong. Giving good context reduces the chance it guesses incorrectly just to fill in missing details.
Alex: So if I'm sitting down to write a prompt, what's my design checklist?
Jordan: Ask yourself five quick questions: What is the task? Who is the output for? What format do I want? What should the model avoid? And what background information does it need?
